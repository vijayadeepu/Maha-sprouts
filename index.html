About MahaSprouts

Why We Sprout Fresh Daily in Bengaluru Instead of Selling Pre-Packed Snacks

Many ready-to-eat snacks in the market rely on day-old pre-cut veggies, heavy fried sev, or cold-chain packets that lose natural crunch and freshness. At MahaSprouts, we soak and sprout whole green moong and brown chana in clean, controlled daily batches tailored for our Bengaluru evening stall window.

Whether you are stepping out of an evening workout, wrapping up a busy workday, or picking up a light, nourishing snack for your family, you get crisp sprouts and fresh garden toppings tossed right before you eat.

What Makes Every MahaSprouts Bowl Special

✓ Balanced Moong + Chana Blend: Combines tender green moong with earthy brown chana for rich texture and plant protein.
✓ Prepared for Evening Freshness: Batches are paced for our evening stall hours so bowls stay crisp and lively.
✓ Tuned to Your Taste: Enjoy classic masala chatpata tang, mild seasoning, or no-onion bowls on request.
